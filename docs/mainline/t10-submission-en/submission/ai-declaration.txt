Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process

OpenAI Codex and AI drafting assistants were used to help draft and revise the Chinese manuscript, translate it into English, translate figure labels, and prepare the submission files. Coding agents also assisted with research implementation, verification scripts, and deterministic typesetting of the existing scientific figures. The English artwork uses the recorded data and geometry of the accepted drawings. The principal investigator approved the Chinese source manuscript. The corresponding author and coauthors are responsible for reviewing the English text, figures, references, code descriptions and declarations and for authorizing the final submission.
