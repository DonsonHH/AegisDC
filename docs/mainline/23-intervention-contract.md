# Authorized, timed named interventions

Ticket23 extends the same eight-domain synthetic V6 facility. `prepare_intervention` consumes only a current public decision frame, declared workforce profile, task, operation catalog and research authorization. Proposal validity is not permission to execute. Fixed sensors remain sparse; historical, manual, work-order and onsite records remain allowed under the existing arrival/provenance rules. No proposal reads actual plant state.

`simulate_scenario` privately consumes actual typed crew completion at its current clock. A completed eligible task needs its full minimum professional work, current authorization in `[starts_at_s, expires_at_s)`, and a setting within the declared physical range. Aborted, rejected, unfinished or expired work produces no successful effect. Work uses the same additive binary64 completion coordinate as the accepted crew executor; subtraction of rounded endpoints must not reject a full declared interval. No time tolerance relaxes authorization.

The closed catalog maps four named operations to one fixed parameter each: network capacity restoration, residual filter cleaning, compute execution request adjustment, and backup cooling request. Callers cannot name arbitrary parameters. No operation resets mass, stored energy, offered service demand or local protection. These are explicit low-order research responses, not certified repair laws. Residual settings can leave loss and uncertainty.

At an event instant, exogenous inputs and existing control/protection settle first, then room/access safety and crew completion. Eligible completed effects apply once, plant and autonomous reserve states settle with `sampled=False`, safety/crew update again, then the snapshot and actual inspection sample occur. This must not grant an extra PLC sample: a backup request at3.5s waits for the4s sample and6s transfer completion. Integration covers the next positive interval only.

Task-local public feedback uses its declared observation and arrival times and shared origin. Full traces, parameter old/new values and private audit remain evaluator outputs. Every intervention feedback carries `risk_closed=False`; completion and symptom change do not close a risk problem. Later actual inspection can observe the residual physical response, and ticket24 owns evidence-verified closure.

The formal engineering protocol freezes eight finite cases and30.25 simulated seconds before execution, uses a unique directory, starts each ledger entry before its call, retains every failure and forbids automatic retries. Durable source, input, tool, raw trace and chart bytes are declared and compared to committed blobs in a fresh checkout. Runtime caches are excluded by type. The two independent original reviews and their decimal-time failure remain preserved alongside a correction supplement. A root integrated full regression is a separate acceptance gate. These fixtures establish neither a policy gain nor an AI/RSI gain; field calibration remains pending and the complete research goal stays active.
