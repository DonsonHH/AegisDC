# Evidence-verified question lifecycle

Ticket24 consumes the accepted current public decision frame, declared question-specific closure rules and arrived task-local intervention feedback. Its public boundary is `assess_closure(frame, program)` and the equivalent module CLI; it never accepts a private plant trace, actual parameter inventory or future record. The existing scenario/decision-frame/CLI testing authorization applies to this evidence-only consumer, following the same pattern as18–20.

Each evaluated question binds an existing risk-catalog question and declares condition and service checks before review. Required numeric bounds, source coverage, post-action observation times, distinct genuine sample origins and a minimum span are explicit synthetic acceptance assumptions. Passing sampled evidence establishes only the declared finite sampled criterion, not continuous behavior between samples or a universal repair guarantee. Current validity and conflicting/missing evidence affect the current lifecycle state. A copy cannot create another confirmation sample.

An APPLIED task feedback proves only the reported authorized action outcome. It cannot close a risk question, invent service recovery, or diagnose the original fault. Fresh condition-only evidence can support symptom retreat while verification remains incomplete. Reaching every declared condition and service criterion permits `VERIFIED_CLOSED` for that question only. The trace cites exact supporting records and their observation, arrival and validity epochs.

Later slices add explicit escalation/risk-acceptance dispositions and evidence-supported false-alarm review. Risk acceptance is an authorized management decision, not physical restoration; an ordinary normal reading never proves a false alarm. Until those slices pass, they remain unavailable rather than fabricated. Other catalog questions remain explicitly unevaluated/open.

Engineering fixtures, independent review and frozen formal saved-output delivery remain separate gates. No policy comparison, field result, AI or RSI benefit is claimed; complete-facility mainline remains active.
