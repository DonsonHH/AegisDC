Ticket 18 binds a finite decision model to the accepted eight-domain V6 public nominal profile. The belief question concerns the dominant impairment around immersion heat rejection and electrical support. In the V6 physical and functional graphs, the lake intake pump, filter and plate heat exchanger participate in heat removal, and electrical supply and rectifier capacity support loads and auxiliaries. ## Public interfaces

`aegis_dc.belief.infer_belief(frame, model)` accepts one closed ticket-15 frame and one separate plain-JSON model. The frame validator reconstructs the ticket-14 view with its observation and arrival gates and verifies its metadata. The CLI `python -m aegis_dc.belief --frame FRAME.json --model MODEL.json` emits one result to standard output. Both inputs are detached from the plant and evaluator. No live or saved actual state, seed, diagnostic truth, future event schedule, or evidence outside the frame is available to inference. The archived engineering harness builds frames separately from the public record archive. Scenario and physical protection code is untouched. Each independent replicate binds an explicit scope ID, ledger ID and nominal-knowledge ID. Run inference separately for each scope.

The closed model declares seconds, dimensionless prior and row-stochastic transition probabilities, state meanings and asset IDs, observation definitions, model variants and their basis. The first variant supplies the reference posterior. V6's checked-in model defines 25 ten-second, left-closed and right-open epochs from zero. A transition occurs at each new epoch, before any factors from that epoch. Coordinates and numeric threshold bounds use exact arithmetic on decoded JSON integers and binary64 numbers. Probability values use their shortest decoded decimal representation and exact rational filtering. Output probabilities are rounded once to binary64 for display. Positive support and impossible epochs are reported from exact arithmetic.

Boolean reports use their declared true/false value. A numeric report with a bounded error bar must fall wholly within one bin, using the declared SI unit and polarity. A range touching a bin boundary from both sides is unresolved. The 2 MW split is an engineering choice for the rectifier channel. The threshold probe bin for a bounded hand-held temperature is 305 K, and the fixed alarm uses its existing public Boolean. Likelihood rows are conditional probabilities of the report bin, given a modeled state and an eligible report. This version does not model availability, report count, missingness, latency, or report-selection as evidence. The ten-second transition probabilities describe changes in the modeled dominant condition and do not execute or imply a repair. With no usable records the model only projects its declared prior.

## Record admission and factor selection

- The ticket-14 frame includes only observations made and arrived by the decision instant. A record's validity end is exclusive. A root that arrives at or after expiry remains audit-only. An eligible root that arrived before expiry can remain a historical factor after expiry, updating its original epoch before projection. A root observed before the scope is audit-only.
- Missing, unknown-quality, unsupported and threshold-straddling roots remain visible in the audit. They reserve no factor slot. JSON false and numeric zero are usable values.
- A copy provides no factor. A missing visible root chain is marked unavailable and is reevaluated when its root arrives. Visible cycles or inconsistent origins are unresolved. Copy references, common origin and declared source-dependency groups form transitive components, including links carried by a record that is itself audit-only. Factors in a provenance-ambiguous component are deferred. Conflicting eligible roots for the same target, observable and observation instant are deferred; the underlying ledger conflict remains visible.
- Candidates are ordered by current availability first, declared channel priority, latest observation time and then record ID. The observed polarity never breaks a tie. A channel and a provenance component each contribute at most one representative across the full scope. The V1 schema permits at most one explicitly declared conditionally independent pair. The reference profile permits the fixed alarm and rectifier pair under distinct acquisition draws conditional on the entire latent path. A visible provenance link still overrides this declaration. Other combinations, including fixed and portable readings of the same heat signal, remain unmodeled and are deferred if they would require multiplying factors. Registry coverage and the number of source IDs do not establish independence.
- Every frame recomputes from the prior. A late root, a changed representative, or a newly visible dependency can replace or remove a previous factor. This represents a change in the retained information set. The report carries original observation time, arrival time, observation epoch, validity, current availability and disposition for each arrived record. The physical-action audit remains empty.

## Posterior, sensitivity and calibration scope

The reference posterior and each variant's positive support are separate from the pointwise minimum/maximum envelope across declared variants. The checked-in variants vary diagnostic discrimination with prior and evolution held fixed. The envelope measures sensitivity to those assumptions and carries no confidence level or sampling-coverage guarantee. If the admitted factors have zero joint support in any variant, report the first impossible epoch for that variant and withhold the envelope. Supported variants are still displayed. Structural non-identifiability, unmodeled evidence and pending field calibration are explicit.

The formal engineering protocol checks exact finite calibration against a separately declared 64-atom generating law. The oracle enumerates two hidden epochs and two binary reports, using independently stated masses and no calls to the public inference implementation. Its report-pattern conditional frequencies and true-law weighted four-class Brier scores are compared to inference from frames carrying only the reports. An archived public-ledger trace exercises integration with real V6 observation outputs. The archive and exact generator have separate scope identities. These checks qualify finite engineering behavior and the stated generator only. Field calibration, risk consequences and inspection value, action execution, and independent mechanism comparison belong to later stages.
