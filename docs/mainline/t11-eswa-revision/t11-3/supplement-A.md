## Reproducing the comparisons from public records

Service deficit integrates the fraction of simultaneous demand unmet over the full window. Unserved quantity is the integral of the absolute shortfall. Time occupied by personnel and tools, issue fees, admitted and refused work, and valid closure are reported on their own scales. A refused request keeps its actual recorded consequences. A modelled report attached to a hypothetical plan never certifies actual closure. Event counts use the sign of unrounded deficit. Keeping all paired arms in the same resampling unit preserves this distinction.

The frozen implementation recorded a common gate at real and hypothetical decision nodes, mandatory verification priority and a safe deferral. It charged tree transitions and terminal evaluations separately. A missing compliant score would leave the full-scope contrast unresolved under the protocol. The release reports 720/720 compliant new positions, 43,920 returned public decisions and zero scientific retries. The study used 63 allocations, 229,800 reserved CPU-seconds and 67,285.886 reported CPU-seconds. These are historical execution totals. The manuscript revision itself submitted zero jobs and made zero facility calls.
