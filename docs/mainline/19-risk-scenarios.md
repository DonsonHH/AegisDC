# Public risk scenarios and finite consequences

Ticket19 exposes `aegis_dc.risk_scenarios.assess_risks(frame, belief_model, catalog)` and `python -m aegis_dc.risk_scenarios --frame FRAME --belief-model MODEL --catalog CATALOG`. Inputs are closed plain JSON. The validated current ticket15 frame and ticket18 inference are the only state information. Caller posteriors, private runs, future observations and physical evaluator handles are not accepted.

## Joint outcome model

The catalog links a finite joint outcome law to the current four-class belief. Each class has a normalized declared distribution over complete effective-capacity trajectories. These are conditional consequence assumptions, not future hidden-state forecasts or a reuse of the belief transition matrix. All outcomes share one exogenous demand curve. Positive capacities, chronological full half-open horizon coverage and the same required compute/network/storage service set are checked. Shared-service delivery is the minimum of offered demand and all required effective component capacities. Unserved normalized work and fractional deficit time remain separate. Zero offered demand contributes zero deficit time. Time and threshold coordinates use exact decoded-number fractions; probability display from ticket18 is converted through shortest decimal representation and renormalized before expectation. The resulting final binary64 comparison uses a frozen1e-14 tolerance. This introduces only a display-rounding correction, not a change in the belief model.

A question names outcomes and a window relative to the current decision time. Its reported consequence is an event-weighted contribution, not a conditional-on-event mean. A portfolio first unions each outcome's overlapping question windows, then integrates and takes expectation. It does not add question losses or multiply marginal event probabilities. Full-horizon unconditional offered/delivered/unserved/deficit accounts are reported separately, including nominal outcomes. These cover the declared finite law only, not every facility cause.

## Eight-domain questions and unknowns

The two quantitative heat/electrical questions retain the bounded ticket18 scope. Six additional domain concerns name reserve switching and common supply, joint IT dependencies, controller/telemetry alternatives, leakage/access, people/tools and room boundaries. Each has actual shared entity/edge references, a distinct conditional impact description, evidence gaps, professional options and closure evidence. The six event probabilities and point consequences are `null`/UNMODELED. Conservative conditional bounds of zero through the full horizon deficit, and zero through common offered work, follow only from nonnegative capacity; they are not field-calibrated risk intervals. Access and crew limitations affect action feasibility rather than inventing immediate service loss. Secondary time effects remain unquantified, separately unit-labeled records. No blanket other-state probability is assigned to all these concerns. Portfolio completeness stays partial when any question is unmodeled.

Reference and sensitivity-variant consequences stay separate. Their min/max range has no confidence interpretation. Impossible evidence in a variant remains a model conflict, never zero risk. A valid reference may still be displayed when another variant conflicts, but the envelope is withheld.

## Options and boundaries

Defer remains available, and direct intervention never requires inspection first. An inspection references an existing public preparation request and consumes `public_feasibility`. An intervention referral declares an operation ID, target, nominal capability interface, qualification, tool set and candidate workers. The interface/target/qualification/tools must match the accepted public nominal capability contract; reported entry restrictions and candidate credentials determine planning eligibility. This check does not authorize a physical operation. All execution permissions remain false, effects are NOT_EXECUTED and dynamic access, positions, tools, occupation, route/timing, authorization and the operation contract remain unresolved. Ticket21 provides actual personnel/resource enforcement,22 inspection production and23 physical effects. Ticket20 compares expected action/inspection value.

FEMA/DHS supply a scenario/impact/capability process reference, not equipment scores or probabilities. [Primary-source applicability](research/19-source-applicability.md) separates established Bayes/expectation/multicomponent maintenance from this engineering mapping and future falsifiable mechanism comparisons. No optimal policy, inspection gain, field calibration or novel decision principle is asserted.

## Engineering evidence

The frozen protocol checks independently stated exact expected accounts, joint-window deduplication and archived08 service-window literals via the public interface. No physical trajectory is generated. Four declared current evidence instants are separate information frames, not research trials. The formal capture pins inputs, tools and all runtime Python before execution, records one attempt before checks, checks HEAD/bytes afterward and preserves failures without overwrite. Saved-output verification checks declared durable files and committed blobs in a fresh checkout; it does not rerun inference or full regression. Root runs the single integrated full suite after review.
